# RP2354A flight controller, rev 0.1.0

A 40 × 40 mm, 2-layer board with:

- **MCU:** RP2354A (2 MB flash in package)
- **IMU:** ICM-42688-P on SPI
- **Inputs:** USB-C, a 2S LiPo through a JST-XH connector, and a GPS connector (JST-GH 4-pin, for a NEO-6M module)
- **Outputs:** 8 servo/ESC 3-pin headers, with SWD on JST-SH
- **Mounting:** M3 holes on a 30.5 mm pattern

The MCU core (regulator, crystal, decoupling, USB) is copied from the Raspberry Pi RP2350A minimal design, including its layout.

## Power

- 2S LiPo (6–8.4 V) or USB powers an AP63203 buck converter, which makes 3.3 V. Each input goes through a Schottky diode, so you can plug in both at once and a reversed battery is blocked.
- The middle pin of every servo header is `SERVO_V`. This rail is **not** connected to the board's supply. Your ESC's BEC powers it, and a servo-only setup needs a separate 5 V source on it.
- The battery voltage reaches the ADC through a 10 k / 3.3 k divider: `VBAT = ADC × 4.03`.

## GPIO map

| Function | GPIO | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| GPS TX/RX | 0 / 1 | UART0 (GPIO0 = MCU TX → GPS RX) |
| OUT1–OUT8 | 4–11 | PWM slices 2–5. OUT1/2, 3/4, 5/6 and 7/8 share a frequency |
| IMU MISO / CS / SCK / MOSI | 16 / 17 / 18 / 19 | SPI0 |
| IMU INT1 | 20 | |
| LED | 25 | Active high |
| VBAT sense | 26 | ADC0 |

## Connectors

| Connector | Pin 1 | Pin 2 | Pin 3 | Pin 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| GPS (JST-GH 4) | 3.3 V | MCU TX → GPS RX | MCU RX ← GPS TX | GND |
| SWD (JST-SH 3, Raspberry Pi debug probe pinout) | SWCLK | GND | SWDIO | |
| 2S+ (JST-XH 2) | VBAT + | GND | | |
| OUT1–8 (2.54 mm) | Signal | SERVO_V (BEC) | GND | |

The GPS connector gives out 3.3 V. GY-NEO6MV2 boards accept that, but their on-board LDO then drops it to about 3.2 V.

To load firmware: hold BOOTSEL while plugging in USB. There's no reset button, so power-cycle the board instead.

## Layout

- `hw/`: the design, edited in KiCad 10. This is the source of truth.
  - `fc.kicad_sch`, `fc.kicad_pcb`: schematic and board.
  - `lib/`: the symbols, footprints and 3D models KiCad doesn't ship. `rpi.*` comes from the Raspberry Pi RP2350A minimal design (MIT licence, in `lib/rpi.pretty/LICENSE.txt`).
  - `fonts/`: IBM Plex Mono, used by the text on the back of the board. Install it (double-click the `.ttf`) before you open or build the board.
- `scripts/build.sh`: checks the design and writes everything to `build/`.
- `pixi.toml`: the Python the build runs on, and its tasks.

The first layout was generated by scripts and an autorouter. They are in history at `336c699`.

## Build

You need KiCad 10 and [pixi](https://pixi.sh).

```sh
pixi run build
```

It runs ERC, DRC with schematic parity, and `scripts/check_layout.py`, and stops on any error before it exports anything. `check_layout.py` checks what DRC cannot: a ground via near every ground pad, the ground pour in one solid piece, no pad fed only by a thin 3.3 V track, and nothing drilled under the IMU.

It writes:

- `build/fc-gerbers.zip`, `build/fc-bom.csv` and `build/fc-cpl.csv` for JLCPCB;
- `build/fc-schematic.pdf`;
- `build/fc.glb`, the assembled board in 3D, for Blender;
- `build/fc-rev<rev>.zip`, the board package: all of the above plus the KiCad files, for descent.

## Viewing the board

Open `build/fc-rev<rev>.zip` in [descent](https://github.com/alvgaona/descent): the 3D model, schematic, PCB, BOM and every file, in the browser. `scripts/package.py` writes the package; its docstring describes the format.

## Ordering (JLCPCB)

- Bare board: upload `fc-gerbers.zip` with 2 layers, 1.6 mm thickness and black solder mask with white silkscreen. The board uses JLCPCB's 2-layer minimum of 0.1 mm track and space, which the RP2350 QFN needs. The smallest via is a 0.25 mm hole in a 0.6 mm pad.
- Assembly: also upload `fc-bom.csv` and `fc-cpl.csv`. They list the 54 SMD parts. The servo headers and the battery connector are through-hole and soldered by hand.
- Check every part's position and rotation in JLCPCB's placement preview before you pay: the CPL has KiCad's rotations, and JLCPCB's differ for some packages.
- The silkscreen has no reference designators. descent's PCB view shows them.
- The ICM-42688-P is an extended part at JLCPCB and has been out of stock at LCSC. Check its stock before ordering.
